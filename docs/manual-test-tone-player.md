# TestTonePlayer Manual Check

Use speakers at a low level when channel isolation is not easy to distinguish on
headphones.

1. Launch TestTonePlayer and confirm at least one output device is listed.
2. Confirm the displayed process ID matches the process in Task Manager.
3. Play the 100 Hz, 440 Hz, and 1000 Hz tones and confirm their pitch increases.
4. Play white noise and confirm continuous broadband noise is audible.
5. Play the 440 Hz pulse and confirm a short burst repeats once per second.
6. For one tone, verify Stereo, Left only, and Right only routing.
7. Move the level control and confirm output changes without playback stopping.
8. Switch output devices while playing and confirm playback moves cleanly.
9. Click Play/Stop rapidly several times and confirm the UI remains responsive.
10. Close the window during playback and confirm the process exits cleanly.

Record the devices and checks performed in the milestone report. Process capture
is intentionally outside this checklist.
